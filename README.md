## What the percentages cost in dollars

A fee quoted as a percentage hides its size. Take a **$10,000 spot trade**:

- Taker buy at 0.10% costs **$10**.
- Sell that position as a maker at 0.08% and you pay **$8**.
- The full round trip costs **$18**, or 0.18% of the position.
- If both sides are taker orders, the round trip is **$20**.

Now scale it. Twenty $10,000 round trips a month costs roughly **$360** at standard rates. That's the number that decides whether a fee schedule is cheap for you, and it's also why the maker/taker split matters: posting a limit order instead of crossing the spread saves 0.02% per side, which is $2 on that same trade.

Withdrawal fees are separate and set per coin and network, so there is no single figure. Check the withdrawal screen for the asset before you move funds.
